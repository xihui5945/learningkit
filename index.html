Spalding Phonograms Support Guide

Directly aligned with the 5-sheet Spalding curriculum. Sounds appear in order of frequency of use. Children are taught the sounds, never the letter names.

📌 Essential Spalding Principles

  • Teach Sounds, Not Letter Names: Call the letter by its sound (/b/, not "bee").
  • Say Multi-Sounds in Frequency Order: e.g., for a say ă, ā, ah (cat, navy, father).
  • English Word Endings: English words do not end with i, u, v, or j. That is why we use ay instead of ai, and oy instead of oi!
  • Single Vowel Rule: ck and dge are only used directly after a single short vowel (e.g. duck, bridge).
